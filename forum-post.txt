[center][b]
WHAT DOES THIS MOD DO?
Once your colonies earn millions a month, a 30k bar mission is not worth the trip.
Missions - Renewed adds contracts in tiers 0 to 7, from pocket money to millions of credits.
Needs LunaLib.
[/b][/center]

[hr][center][size=12pt][b]Contracts in the bar[/b][/size][/center][hr]
[list]
[li]Every bar has a chance (50 percent by default) to offer a contract. The bar option shows its tier and job type, for example "Look over the contract (tier 5, hauling)".[/li]
[li]Tiers are random, but lean a little toward your fleet size, colony income and credits. A late-game fleet sees fewer tier 0 jobs, and every tier stays possible.[/li]
[li]Each contract pays an advance when you sign and the rest on completion.[/li]
[li]Missing the deadline or abandoning the contract charges a penalty, even if that leaves you in debt, and costs reputation with the client's faction.[/li]
[li]You can hold 3 contracts at once. They are in the intel screen under Missions.[/li]
[/list]

[hr][center][size=12pt][b]Job types[/b][/size][/center][hr]
[list]
[li][b]Hauling:[/b] the client hands you the cargo: ore, metals, food, machinery and other trade goods. Deliver all of it to the named market before the deadline. Running off with the cargo costs more than it is worth.[/li]
[li][b]Fuel run:[/b] the client fills your fuel tanks. Arrive with the full amount, so every jump on the way burns into the delivery unless you bring spare fuel.[/li]
[li][b]Crew transport:[/b] contract workers ride in your crew quarters. Crew lost in battle count against the delivery.[/li]
[li][b]Contribution:[/b] bring goods you find or buy yourself to a construction project. At tiers 4 to 6 the project may ask for a colony item instead.[/li]
[li][b]Investment:[/b] put up a stake and get it back with profit after 5 to 12 months. Pulling out early returns half the stake. Pirates and the Luddic Path do not offer them.[/li]
[li]Docking at the destination completes a delivery job.[/li]
[li]Tier 0 hauling asks for 100 to 300 units and pays 5k to 20k. Tier 7 asks for 12,000 to 20,000 units and pays 3M to 4M.[/li]
[/list]

[hr][center][size=12pt][b]Planned[/b][/size][/center][hr]
[list]
[li]Combat contracts. From tier 5 the enemies are Omega, Shrouded Dwellers, Threat and hand-built fleets, never pirates.[/li]
[li]Marine operations and expeditions.[/li]
[/list]

[hr][center][size=12pt][b]Settings and compatibility[/b][/size][/center][hr]
[list]
[li]LunaLib settings: chance of an offer per bar, how much the tier roll leans toward your strength, reward multiplier.[/li]
[li]Test mode in LunaLib, off by default: spawn any job type at any tier at any bar, and complete or fail open contracts at once.[/li]
[li]Does not take a place from other mods' bar events.[/li]
[li]Can be added to a game in progress. Removing it from a game in progress breaks that save.[/li]
[/list]
