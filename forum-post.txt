[center][b]
WHAT DOES THIS MOD DO?
Once your colonies earn millions a month, a 30k bar mission is not worth the trip.
Missions - Renewed adds contracts in tiers 0 to 7, from pocket money to millions of credits.
Needs LunaLib.
[/b][/center]

[hr][center][size=12pt][b]Contracts in the bar[/b][/size][/center][hr]
[list]
[li]Every bar has a chance (50 percent by default) to have a mission board. "Open the mission board" lists 1 to 5 contracts by market size, each with its own tier and job type, for example "Tier 5 hauling": 1 or 2 at size 4 and 5, 2 or 3 at size 6, 3 or 4 at size 7, 4 or 5 at size 8, always 5 at size 9 and up. Size 3 markets rarely have a board, with 1 contract. Smaller markets have none.[/li]
[li]Tiers are random, but lean a little toward your fleet size, colony income and credits. A late-game fleet sees fewer tier 0 jobs, and every tier stays possible.[/li]
[li]Each contract pays an advance when you sign and the rest on completion.[/li]
[li]Missing the deadline or abandoning the contract charges a penalty, even if that leaves you in debt, and costs reputation with the client's faction.[/li]
[li]You can hold 3 contracts at once. They are in the intel screen under Missions.[/li]
[/list]

[hr][center][size=12pt][b]Job types[/b][/size][/center][hr]
[list]
[li][b]Hauling:[/b] the client hands you the cargo: ore, metals, food, machinery and other trade goods. Deliver all of it to the named market before the deadline. Running off with the cargo costs more than it is worth.[/li]
[li][b]Fuel run:[/b] the client fills your fuel tanks. Arrive with the full amount, so every jump on the way burns into the delivery unless you bring spare fuel.[/li]
[li][b]Crew transport:[/b] contract workers ride in your crew quarters. Crew lost in battle count against the delivery.[/li]
[li][b]Contribution:[/b] bring goods you find or buy yourself to a construction project. At tiers 4 to 6 the project may ask for a colony item instead.[/li]
[li][b]Investment:[/b] put up a stake and get it back with profit after 5 to 12 months. Pulling out early returns half the stake. Pirates and the Luddic Path do not offer them.[/li]
[li][b]Combat:[/b] someone was attacked near a planet in a nearby system and barely got away. Find the fleet that did it and destroy it.[/li]
[li][b]Marine operation:[/b] raid a market hostile to the client to knock out a named industry, or bring your own marines to a garrison.[/li]
[li][b]Expedition:[/b] haul supplies to a camp in an uncolonised system. Something may be waiting there.[/li]
[li][b]Defense:[/b] a fleet will attack a market of the client's faction in a set number of days. Be there and destroy it. The market's station fights with you.[/li]
[li][b]Famous ship lead:[/b] pay for the location of one named ship, sized by the tier: a pristine derelict to recover, or the flagship of a hostile fleet you can always recover once it is beaten.[/li]
[li][b]Ship deal:[/b] a client pays more than its value for a given hull you bring to their bar, or sells you a pristine ship below its value.[/li]
[li][b]Conquest:[/b] capture a market of a faction hostile to the client. Tier 3 and up, needs Nexerelin.[/li]
[li][b]Salvage lead:[/b] pay for the location of a debris field. The loot grows with the tier.[/li]
[li]Docking at the destination completes a delivery job.[/li]
[li]Clients you satisfy may offer to become contacts, more often at higher tiers.[/li]
[li]Tier 0 hauling asks for 100 to 300 units and pays 1.5k to 6.5k. Tier 7 asks for 24,000 to 40,000 units and pays 1M to 1.3M. Hauling, fuel runs and crew transport pay a third of other jobs of the same tier, and combat pays less from tier 4 (1.2M to 1.6M at tier 7). Enemy fleets grow from about 10 DP at tier 0 to about 500 DP at tier 7.[/li]
[/list]

[hr][center][size=12pt][b]Settings and compatibility[/b][/size][/center][hr]
[list]
[li]LunaLib settings: chance of a mission board per bar, how much the tier roll leans toward your strength, lowest and highest tier, each job type on or off, reward multiplier, a pay multiplier for each job type, enemy strength multiplier.[/li]
[li]Debug section in LunaLib with a test mode, off by default: spawn any job type at any tier at any bar, and complete or fail open contracts at once.[/li]
[li]Does not take a place from other mods' bar events.[/li]
[li]Can be added to a game in progress. Removing it from a game in progress breaks that save.[/li]
[/list]
