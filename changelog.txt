Version 0.8.2 (2026-11-13)
- Famous ship leads pick by price: all warships are sorted by value and split into 8 equal groups, tier 0 the cheapest and tier 7 the most expensive, and the ship is drawn at random from its tier's group. One very expensive modded ship no longer pushes every tier up.
- Famous ships always have S-mods: 1 at tiers 0 to 2, 2 at tiers 3 to 5, 3 at tiers 6 and 7.
- A famous ship lead never costs more than half of what a contract of its tier pays.
- Proving yourself opens tiers two at a time: tiers 0 and 1 are open from the start, 3 contracts of tier 0 or higher open 2 and 3, 3 of tier 2 or higher open 4 and 5, and 3 of tier 4 or higher open 6 and 7. New LunaLib setting for how many tiers open at once.
- With Tahlan Shipworks, the Legio Infernalis turns up wherever pirates do.

Version 0.8.1 (2026-11-12)
- Tier 5 to 7 combat, defense and expedition enemies are now mostly elite war fleets of a faction at war with the client. Anything stranger is very rare.
- Famous ship leads bring better warships at higher tiers: no freighters or transports, and no pirate or Luddic Path refits from tier 4.
- Ship sales: most ships for sale are used, with 1 to 4 D-mods and a lower price. Pristine ships are rare.
- Investment terms read "Risk: Safe" (or Guaranteed, Risky).
- Expedition terms say who is taking an interest in the route and how much, for example "Pirate interest: Severe".

Version 0.8.0 (2026-11-12)
- Offers show the ship on the right for ship deals and famous ship leads.
- Combat and defense offers show a glimpse of the enemy fleet: its biggest ships and an estimate of its size in DP.
- Ship deals: instead of ordering a hull for later, a client now asks for one ship from your fleet and pays well over its value on the spot. With Starship Legends, well-known ships fetch more.
- Defense: the attacking fleet appears as soon as you sign and waits outside the market. It attacks when you arrive in the system.
- Investments: no more pulling out early. Each venture is guaranteed, safe or risky, in the client's words. Safe ventures rarely fail and return half the stake; risky ones pay much more but can lose all of it.
- Conquest: from tier 5 only. Tier 5 asks for a size 3 market, tier 6 size 4, tier 7 size 5. Story markets such as Chicomoztoc and Gilead are never targets.
- Salvage leads: more colony items, AI cores, hull mods and blueprints at higher tiers, and a few battered scavenger ships guard the field.
- Expeditions say how likely an ambush is in words (unlikely, possible, likely, almost certain) instead of a percentage.
- Offers are written in the client's own voice.
- New LunaLib setting "Proving yourself", on by default: clients offer work one tier above what you have done. Finish 3 contracts of a tier (or higher) to open the next one; the number is a setting too. Buying or selling ships and investments do not count.

Version 0.7.1 (2026-11-12)
- Contracts per mission board now follow market size: 1 or 2 at size 4 and 5, 2 or 3 at size 6, 3 or 4 at size 7, 4 or 5 at size 8, always 5 at size 9 and up. Size 3 markets rarely have a board, with 1 contract. Smaller markets have none.

Version 0.7.0 (2026-11-12)
- New mission board: one "Open the mission board" option at the bar lists 2 to 5 contracts, more at bigger markets, each with its own tier and job type. Signing one leaves the others on the board.
- Ship hand-overs and the test mode spawner moved onto the board.

Version 0.6.0 (2026-11-11)
- New job type: defense. A fleet will attack a market of the client's faction in a set number of days. Be there and destroy it.
- New job type: famous ship leads. Pay for the location of one named ship sized by the tier: a pristine derelict to recover, or the flagship of a hostile fleet.
- New job type: ship deals. A client pays well for a hull you bring to their bar, or sells you a pristine ship below its value.
- New job type: conquest, tier 3 and up. Capture a market of a faction hostile to the client. Needs Nexerelin.
- New job type: salvage leads. Pay for the location of a debris field with loot by tier.
- LunaLib switches for the new job types, and pay settings for defense, ship orders, conquest and salvage lead loot.

Version 0.4.1 (2026-11-10)
- Fixed: after your first contract in a play session, opening the next offer at any bar closed it at once.
- Threat and Shrouded Dweller contract fleets are now built like the game's own: no AI core captains, the game's Threat battle and loot rules, and Dweller drops.

Version 0.4.0 (2026-11-10)
- New job type: combat. Break a hostile fleet waiting near a planet in a nearby system. Tiers 0 to 4 send pirates or Pathers. From tier 5 the enemy is an Omega, Threat or Shrouded Dweller fleet, or Abyssal and Dustkeeper fleets when their mods are installed.
- New job type: marine operations. Raid a market hostile to the client to knock out a named industry, or bring your own marines to a garrison of the client's faction.
- New job type: expeditions. Haul supplies to a camp at a planet in an uncolonised system. The offer shows the chance of an ambush waiting there.
- New job type: smuggling. Carry weapons, drugs or organs to a market where they are illegal. Patrols that scan you take the goods and your standing.
- Clients you satisfy may offer to become contacts, more often at higher tiers.
- New LunaLib switches for the four new job types, and an enemy strength multiplier.

Version 0.3.0 (2026-11-10)
- New LunaLib switches turn each job type on or off.
- New LunaLib sliders set the lowest and highest tier bars offer. Offers already rolled outside the new settings are hidden until the bar refreshes.
- Test mode moved to its own Debug section in LunaLib. It ignores the job type and tier settings.

Version 0.2.0 (2026-11-10)
- New job type: fuel runs. The client fills your fuel tanks and you deliver the full amount. Fuel burned on the way comes out of the delivery.
- New job type: crew transport. Contract workers ride in your crew quarters to another market. Crew lost in battle count against the delivery.
- New job type: contributions. Bring goods you find or buy yourself to a construction project at another market. At tiers 4 to 6 the project may ask for a colony item instead.
- New job type: investments. Put up a stake and get it back with profit after 5 to 12 months. Pulling out early returns half the stake. Pirates and the Luddic Path do not offer them.
- New LunaLib test mode: every bar can spawn any job type at any tier, and open contracts get buttons to complete or fail them at once. Off by default.
- Fixed: a bar with no contract on your last visit could hide its next contract for up to 40 days.

Version 0.1.0 (2026-11-09)
- First release.
- Every bar has a chance (50 percent by default) to offer a tiered contract, tier 0 to 7, with the tier and job type shown in the bar.
- The tier roll leans a little toward your fleet size, colony income and credits. A LunaLib setting controls how much, or turns it off.
- First job type: hauling. The client hands over the cargo, you deliver it to another market before the deadline.
- Contracts pay an advance on signing and the rest on delivery. Missing the deadline or abandoning the contract charges a penalty, even into debt, and costs reputation with the client's faction.
- At most 3 open contracts at once.
