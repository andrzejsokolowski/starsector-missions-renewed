Version 0.3.0 (2026-11-10)
- New LunaLib switches turn each job type on or off.
- New LunaLib sliders set the lowest and highest tier bars offer. Offers already rolled outside the new settings are hidden until the bar refreshes.
- Test mode moved to its own Debug section in LunaLib. It ignores the job type and tier settings.

Version 0.2.0 (2026-11-10)
- New job type: fuel runs. The client fills your fuel tanks and you deliver the full amount. Fuel burned on the way comes out of the delivery.
- New job type: crew transport. Contract workers ride in your crew quarters to another market. Crew lost in battle count against the delivery.
- New job type: contributions. Bring goods you find or buy yourself to a construction project at another market. At tiers 4 to 6 the project may ask for a colony item instead.
- New job type: investments. Put up a stake and get it back with profit after 5 to 12 months. Pulling out early returns half the stake. Pirates and the Luddic Path do not offer them.
- New LunaLib test mode: every bar can spawn any job type at any tier, and open contracts get buttons to complete or fail them at once. Off by default.
- Fixed: a bar with no contract on your last visit could hide its next contract for up to 40 days.

Version 0.1.0 (2026-11-09)
- First release.
- Every bar has a chance (50 percent by default) to offer a tiered contract, tier 0 to 7, with the tier and job type shown in the bar.
- The tier roll leans a little toward your fleet size, colony income and credits. A LunaLib setting controls how much, or turns it off.
- First job type: hauling. The client hands over the cargo, you deliver it to another market before the deadline.
- Contracts pay an advance on signing and the rest on delivery. Missing the deadline or abandoning the contract charges a penalty, even into debt, and costs reputation with the client's faction.
- At most 3 open contracts at once.
